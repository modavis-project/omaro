# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community.
Participants must communicate constructively, respect cultural and disciplinary
differences, cite others' work, and avoid demeaning or discriminatory behavior.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, threats, unwanted sexual
attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publication of
private information without permission.

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer through the repository's
security contact mechanism. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation,
or take other proportionate action. Reports will be handled confidentially and
without retaliation. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing
the project elsewhere.
